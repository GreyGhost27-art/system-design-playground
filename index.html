⌨ Shortcuts
  • Drag a component onto the board
  • Drag from a port to run a cable
  • Drag a cable near its end (or Shift+drag a port) to unplug it
  • Click a cable to set its traffic or label it
  • Double-click a component to read about it
  • Del removes the selection
  • Ctrl+C / V copies a component
  • Ctrl+Z undoes the last change
  • Drag a group by its name to move it with everything in it
  • Scroll zooms · drag the board to pan
